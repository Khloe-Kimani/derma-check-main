Why This Was Built

Skincare should be about confidence, not confusion.

Understanding Your Skincare Should Be Easy

Ever had to go through lines of unreadable text at the back of a new skincare product just to understand what's inside?

We created DermaCheck to make skincare ingredient information easier to understand and more accessible.

This Website Aims To:

  • Make ingredient lists understandable.
  • Personalize ingredient safety checks.
  • Support people with skin sensitivities.
  • Help users identify potential allergens.
  • Build confidence in skincare choices.
