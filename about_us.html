Who We Are

A platform built for clarity

We are a skincare ingredient analysis platform designed to help individuals understand the ingredients found in their skincare products. Our platform allows users to scan products and identify ingredients that may contain potential allergens or substances that could trigger skin sensitivities.

We believe everyone deserves access to clear, understandable, and reliable skincare ingredient information to make more informed choices about the products they use every day.

Our mission and vision

  • 01 Mission — Make skincare ingredient information accessible and understandable to everyone. We aim to empower individuals to identify potential allergens, understand product ingredients, and make informed skincare decisions that support their individual skin needs.
  • 02 Vision — A world where no one is caught off guard by what's in their skincare, where checking a label is as quick and clear as reading a nutrition fact sheet, for every skin type and sensitivity.
